ionic_conductivity_step.ionic_conductivity module#

Non-graphical part of the Ionic Conductivity step in a SEAMM flowchart

The ionic conductivity of an electrolyte from equilibrium MD with fixed charges: by Einstein-Helfand, from the collective displacement of the ions’ charges, σ = lim ⟨‖P(t) − P(0)‖²⟩ / (6 V kB t) with P = Σ qᵢ rᵢ over the ions; by Nernst-Einstein, from the ions’ self-diffusion; and their ratio, the ionicity. The analysis is in seamm_md_analysis.electric.

The MD is run before this step – in a Loop for independent runs – or read from files; the step analyzes it.

ionic_conductivity_step.ionic_conductivity.A2_PER_PS = 1e-08#

Å²/ps in m²/s

ionic_conductivity_step.ionic_conductivity.D_UNIT = 1e-09#

The unit of the self-diffusion coefficients in the output, m²/s

ionic_conductivity_step.ionic_conductivity.formal_charges(configuration)[source]#

The formal charge of each atom of the structure, or None if it has none.

For an MD without fixed charges (a machine-learned forcefield): the ions’ net charges are all the conductivity needs, and where the charge of an ion sits within it does not matter for its displacement.
